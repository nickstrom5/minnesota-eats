Classic counters & icons

15 places: diners, delis, cafés and family restaurants that have fed Minnesota for decades. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source or Visit Saint Paul's own pages.

Icons with a past

Visit Saint Paul's list of the city's icons runs from Mickey's, the dining car downtown that harks back to 1939, to Boca Chica (since 1964), El Burrito Mercado (since 1979) and Cecil's deli, open more than 70 years. Tasting Table (February 2026) writes that Minneapolis could never let go of Kramarczuk's, an old-world deli at the same address since 1967. In September 2025, Tasting Table names it Minnesota's oldest restaurant still open: the Hubbell House in Mantorville.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: St. Paul (11), Minneapolis (1), Hampton (1), Mantorville (1), St. Cloud (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or a visitor bureau's own page or listing for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.

Twin Cities

13 places, by town.

  1. Dan's Bar and Grill

    8485 240th St E, Hampton

    Classic counter

    A long-running burger spot in New Trier; the Buddha burger has two cheeses and mushrooms.

  2. Kramarczuk's Sausage

    215 E Hennepin Ave, Minneapolis

    America's ClassicClassic counter

    An old-world Ukrainian deli, bakery and cafeteria at the same address since 1967: house sausages, pierogi and cabbage rolls.

  3. Boca Chica Restaurante Mexicano y Cantina

    11 Cesar Chavez St, St. Paul

    Classic counter Since 1964

    A family-run West Side Mexican restaurant since 1964, known for carnitas and enchiladas.

  4. Cafe Latte

    850 Grand Ave, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    Grand Avenue's cafeteria-style comfort food for 40 years, with a pizza and wine bar.

  5. Cecils Deli & Restaurant

    651 Cleveland Ave S, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    Highland Park's deli for more than 70 years: pastrami on rye and matzo ball soup.

  6. DeGidio's Restaurant & Bar, St. Paul

    425 7th St W, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    Red-sauce pasta and the old-school hot dago, with roots going back to Prohibition.

  7. El Burrito Mercado

    175 Cesar Chavez St, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    The West Side's Mexican market since 1979, with a deli, restaurant and cantina.

  8. Mancini's Char House

    531 7th St W, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    West Seventh's steakhouse with a 1970s bar and the pickle plate.

  9. Mickey's Diner

    36 7th St W, St. Paul

    Classic counter Since 1939

    The 1939 dining car downtown, reopened in October 2024: hash browns, smashed burgers and shakes.

  10. Moscow on the Hill

    371 Selby Ave, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    Russian and Ukrainian comfort food since 1994: stroganoff and pelmeni.

  11. Sweeney's Saloon

    96 Dale St N, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    A neighborhood pub since 1979 with an Art Deco bar, wings and hard-shell tacos.

  12. W.A. Frost & Company

    374 Selby Ave #1825, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    New American dining in Cathedral Hill since 1975, with a shaded patio.

  13. Yarusso Bros Italian Restaurant

    635 Payne Ave, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    Payne Avenue Italian from a family that opened its first tavern in 1932, with early bocce lanes.

Central Minnesota

1 place, by town.

  1. Val's Rapid Service

    628 E St Germain St, St. Cloud

    Classic counter

    Val's: cheap burgers (cheeseburgers under $2.50) and a drive-up chocolate shake.

Southern Minnesota

1 place, by town.

  1. The Hubbell House

    502 N Main St, Mantorville

    Classic counter

    Tasting Table names it Minnesota's oldest restaurant still open: the stagecoach-era hotel Frank Mantor built in Mantorville.
