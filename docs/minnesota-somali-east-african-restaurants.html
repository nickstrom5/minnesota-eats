Somali & East African

5 places for Somali and East African cooking in the Twin Cities. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

Ask for early accessThe free Minnesota Eats app for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store.

Minneapolis and St. Paul

Sahan Journal (November 2026) profiles Abdirahman Kahin, who built Afro Deli into three Twin Cities locations over 16 years, and Sadiq Ali's Deg Deg Grill, open since 2009 at Karmel Mall and the 24 Mall in south Minneapolis. On St. Paul's East Side, Visit Saint Paul points to Karibu Grocery & Deli for East African and Afro-Mediterranean plates.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Minneapolis (3), St. Paul (2).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or a visitor bureau's own page or listing for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
