Minnesota James Beard restaurants

22 Minnesota restaurants honored by the James Beard Foundation (3 America's Classics, 3 finalists, 9 semifinalists and 7 winners). No MICHELIN Guide covers Minnesota.

What counts as an honor here

One source, reported as facts rather than scores: the James Beard Foundation. Its America's Classics went to Al's Breakfast (2004), the Pickwick in Duluth (2007) and Kramarczuk's (2013); its Best Chef: Midwest winners run from Alex Roberts of Alma (2010) to Karyn Tomlinson of Myriel (2025); Best New Restaurant went to Owamni (2022), now Indígena by Owamni at the Guthrie Theater, and to Bûcheron (2025); and there are the 2025 and 2026 finalists and semifinalists. Where an honor went to a chef, the line under the place names them.

James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation. Minnesota Eats is not affiliated with it.
