Hmong & Southeast Asian

8 places for Hmong, Lao, Thai and Vietnamese cooking in St. Paul. Each was checked in fall 2026 against Visit Saint Paul's own pages.

St. Paul's markets and University Avenue

Visit Saint Paul points to two Hmong markets: Hmongtown Marketplace, with 11 restaurants among its more than 125 stores, and Hmong Village on the East Side, with more than 20 food stalls. Along University Avenue are Trung Nam French Bakery and Bangkok Thai Deli; Hoa Bien has served Vietnamese food in Frogtown since 1978, and Ruam Mit has cooked Thai and Lao recipes downtown for more than 35 years.
