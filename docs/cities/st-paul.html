St. Paul restaurants: classic diners and icons and more

What St. Paul has on our lists: 11 classic counters and icons, 8 Hmong or Southeast Asian kitchens, 4 James Beard honorees, 3 Juicy Lucy bars, 2 Somali or East African kitchens and 1 of Minnesota's oldest restaurants (from 1939). Classic counters & icons in St. Paul

Each one checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source. For all of Minnesota: 15 classic counters and icons.

  1. Mickey's Diner

    36 7th St W, St. Paul

    Classic counter Since 1939

    The 1939 dining car downtown, reopened in October 2024: hash browns, smashed burgers and shakes.

  2. Boca Chica Restaurante Mexicano y Cantina

    11 Cesar Chavez St, St. Paul

    Classic counter Since 1964

    A family-run West Side Mexican restaurant since 1964, known for carnitas and enchiladas.

  3. Cafe Latte

    850 Grand Ave, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    Grand Avenue's cafeteria-style comfort food for 40 years, with a pizza and wine bar.

  4. Cecils Deli & Restaurant

    651 Cleveland Ave S, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    Highland Park's deli for more than 70 years: pastrami on rye and matzo ball soup.

  5. DeGidio's Restaurant & Bar, St. Paul

    425 7th St W, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    Red-sauce pasta and the old-school hot dago, with roots going back to Prohibition.

  6. El Burrito Mercado

    175 Cesar Chavez St, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    The West Side's Mexican market since 1979, with a deli, restaurant and cantina.

  7. Mancini's Char House

    531 7th St W, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    West Seventh's steakhouse with a 1970s bar and the pickle plate.

  8. Moscow on the Hill

    371 Selby Ave, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    Russian and Ukrainian comfort food since 1994: stroganoff and pelmeni.

  9. Sweeney's Saloon

    96 Dale St N, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    A neighborhood pub since 1979 with an Art Deco bar, wings and hard-shell tacos.

  10. W.A. Frost & Company

    374 Selby Ave #1825, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    New American dining in Cathedral Hill since 1975, with a shaded patio.

  11. Yarusso Bros Italian Restaurant

    635 Payne Ave, St. Paul

    Classic counter

    Payne Avenue Italian from a family that opened its first tavern in 1932, with early bocce lanes.

  5. Ruam Mit

  6. Sawatdee Saint Paul

    486 N Robert St, St. Paul

  8. Trung Nam French Bakery
